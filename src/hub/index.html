Hub: Your Work Organised

One page for each project: its files and links, Claude sessions, tasks, notes, meetings, emails, deadlines and the time you spend on it. For the Mac.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

Drag an email straight from Outlook

Drag an email out of Outlook, or Apple Mail, and drop it on a project's page. Hub saves a copy in your emails folder and keeps its sender, date and text on the page, where you can read and search it. Emails downloaded from Gmail come in the same way.

A focus clock and a calculator at hand

The focus clock times a spell of work, then a break. The calculator opens in its own small window beside your work. Both are one click away in the toolbar.

Files and links where they are

Drag files, folders and web links onto a project's page. Hub links to them in place; it does not move or copy your files.

Tasks, notes, meetings and emails

Each project keeps its next step, its tasks and sub-tasks, notes, meetings (including calendar invitations), emails from Mail, Outlook or Gmail (Hub keeps a copy in a folder you choose) and a dated log of what was done.

Deadlines and Task Central

Give a file, link, note, email or task a deadline, a few words on what it is for and a reminder before it. Home and the Calendar show what is due; Task Central lists your projects' tasks in one window.

Time

A focus clock, a timer for each project and a record of the time spent.

Claude

If you use the Claude app or Claude Code, Hub lists the sessions run in a project's folder and can start a new one with a short briefing about the project.

On your Mac

Each project is one small file on your Mac. Hub has no account, no adverts and no analytics, and it does not send your projects anywhere.

Then a monthly or yearly plan through the App Store

Hub shows each plan's price, in your own currency, before you subscribe. The free month is for new subscribers, and a plan renews until you cancel it in your App Store account. When a subscription ends, everything you have put in Hub stays yours to open and use.
