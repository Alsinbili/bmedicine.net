Licence

Hub from the Mac App Store is licensed to you under Apple's Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement. These pages explain how Hub's subscription works. They do not replace Apple's agreement.

The subscription

  • Plans. Monthly, at £4.99 a month, or yearly, at £49 a year (in the United Kingdom; in other countries the App Store shows the price in your currency).
  • The free month. Each plan starts with one free month for new subscribers, once per Apple Account. Nothing is charged during the free month.
  • Renewal. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. A plan renews automatically at the same price at the end of each month or year, including at the end of the free month, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Cancelling and changing. Cancel or change your plan at any time in your App Store account settings (in Hub: Settings, General, Subscription, Manage Subscription). Cancelling stops the next renewal; your plan continues until the end of the period already paid.
  • Refunds. Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Codes. Sometimes I give codes, for example for a discount for a number of months. A code's offer, its length and who may use it are set out when it is given. Redeem a code in Hub (Redeem Code…) or through its link.

When a subscription ends

Hub keeps working with everything already in it. You can still open every hub, file, link, email and session, change what is there (statuses, colours, stars, reminders, next steps, names), tick tasks, write notes, add to a project's log, remove things, use the focus clock and the calculator, and make backups and the iCloud copy. Adding new hubs, files, links, Claude sessions, tasks, meetings and emails needs a subscription. Nothing you have put in Hub is locked away.

Your projects

Your hubs are files on your Mac, and they are yours. Hub links to your files where they are; it does not move or change them, with two exceptions you ask for: an email you drag in from a mail app is saved as a copy in your emails folder, and an email you download from Gmail is moved from Downloads into that folder. Keep backups of anything important; Hub can make a full backup for you (Settings, Backups).
